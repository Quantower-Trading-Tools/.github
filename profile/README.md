Quantower includes charting and technical analysis features that support different research routines. Users can work with available indicators, drawing tools, and chart settings to examine trends, price levels, and other market characteristics.

Market analysis workflows can include features such as volume analysis, heatmap-style visualizations, and other specialized tools where supported. These features can provide additional context when reviewing price movements and trading activity.

Quantower also offers tools for organizing trading activity and monitoring selected market conditions. Available alerts, order management options, and related features may vary according to the platform configuration and connected services.

For users working with futures, forex, options, or digital asset markets, the platform's supported connections can provide a centralized environment for charting and market research. Availability should be confirmed with the relevant broker or provider.

Workspace customization helps users maintain a consistent analysis process. By organizing relevant charts, market depth panels, and analytical tools, users can reduce repetitive setup work during regular market reviews.

The platform's capabilities depend on compatible hardware, a stable network connection, suitable account permissions, and access to the relevant market data. Users should consult the current product documentation for connection-specific requirements.
